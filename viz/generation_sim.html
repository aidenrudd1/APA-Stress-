Plugged In, Then and Now

Each dot is one real survey respondent, colored by generation. As the questions change, people move to the answer they gave. The left field is the 2018 survey and the right is 2024, so you can watch how each generation sorts itself on social media, screens, mental health and stress.

Who answered what

Each field shows 800 adults drawn from that year's survey in proportion to the survey weights, so one dot stands for about 1 in 800 U.S. adults. The 2018 and 2024 dots are different people. The percentages on the fields and in the table come from the full weighted surveys (2018: 3,858 adults; 2024: 3,936).

Generations use fixed birth years: Gen Z 1997–2012, Millennials 1981–1996, Gen X 1965–1980, Boomers & older 1964 and earlier. In 2018 the Gen Z adults were 18–21; by 2024 they were 18–27. Questions marked "different question" cover a similar topic, but the wording or answer format changed between surveys.

"Not on social media" in 2018 means the person answered "not applicable". In 2024 it means they ticked "I do not use social media".
